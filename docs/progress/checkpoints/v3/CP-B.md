## Main findings for C to evaluate

- Actual destination readiness, visual pacing and arrival are separate. A visual Skip is not automatically operation cancellation. Historical response-time heuristics are not selected Orbit timing budgets.
- Original Multiverse/Dimensions art can use projection, geometry and representational color vocabulary without claiming scientific proof. Rank/status information still needs readable, non-color meaning; typography, motion and sound must support controls rather than obscure them.
- Infinite animation repetition is not unbounded world streaming. Procedural fields, bounded active regions, pool reset/exhaustion, shared-resource lifetime, coordinate precision and reverse regeneration require separate design and tests.
- Competition evidence distinguishes activity from intrinsic motivation. The opened 427-participant study used fictitious static rankings; it cannot justify fabricated product ranks, guarantee retention or settle cohort/score policy.
- Reduced motion, flashing, automatic updates, focus order and status announcements have different requirements and exceptions. W3C Understanding pages are informative explanations, not a declaration of WCAG conformance.
