## 3. Ten key findings or decisions so far

External source URLs, access dates and exact locators are retained in the linked notes/register. Observed source statements, methodological inference and provisional advice are not collapsed together.

1. **Rating uncertainty requires a model suited to the scale.** Binary Wilson bounds and binary Beta approaches are not formulas for an ordinary five-star mean. The opened star-rating article supplies a separate Dirichlet/normal approximation, not approved product weights or proof of fraud resistance. [Ranking evidence](../../plan/v2/research-marketplace-economy.md).
2. **Matching, notification delivery and claim ownership need separate contracts.** The allocator efficiency, new-mentor fairness and duplicate-effort questions were not established by blocked marketplace pages. Do not pretend a ranked alert or analogy proves the final allocation model. [C01 gaps](../../plan/v2/research-marketplace-economy.md).
3. **Engagement is not learning or wellbeing.** Detailed notification and product-habit reports support bounded activity claims, not notification-frequency safety, mastery gains or transferable causal effects on this platform. [Marketplace](../../plan/v2/research-marketplace-economy.md), [learning](../../plan/v2/research-learning-evidence.md).
4. **Accounting guarantees differ.** Same-ID retries, authorization, semantic reward deduplication, atomic chains, persistent linkage, corrections and sustainable issuance are separate issues. Five ledger pages from one vendor do not establish inflation targets or a chosen database. [C04 evidence](../../plan/v2/research-marketplace-economy.md).
5. **Canvas license and product scope matter.** Basic Fabric/Konva/Yjs/Automerge/Excalidraw license evidence is available; tldraw source availability does not make commercial production unrestricted. A drawing core is not automatically an authorized, durable collaborative service. [Whiteboard evidence](../../plan/v2/research-media-whiteboard.md).
6. **Crash-safe recording and resumable upload are separate.** Chunk callbacks can be delayed and are not the media clock. Network upload protocols cannot recover local bytes that were never durably saved. Full browser/OS, editing, captions and replay comparisons remain gaps. [Media evidence](../../plan/v2/research-media-whiteboard.md).
7. **Editors and generated drafts are not complete notes products.** Core versus advanced commercial modules, UI/storage, transcript anchors, export fidelity and factual grounding need separate checks. Local inference or mentor approval alone is not an accuracy guarantee. [Notes evidence](../../plan/v2/research-notes-calls.md).
8. **Call prices are not interchangeable headline minutes.** Participant connection, recording tracks/wall-clock, composition, bandwidth/storage and free pools differ. Self-hosting moves operations responsibility, not cost to zero; no India reliability winner is established. [Call comparison](../../plan/v2/research-notes-calls.md).
9. **India compliance is still a blocker for final policy/money design.** Selected RBI text and actual DPDP provisions were read, but tax/withholding, full commencement/amendment/current applicability, provider contracts and operational child safeguards remain unresolved. Enactment, publication and effect are distinct; an education label is not a blanket exemption. [India/safety evidence](../../plan/v2/research-india-safety.md).
10. **The extra-feature evidence is real but concentrated.** 33 coded problem observations meet the numeric pain-point target after explicit deduplication, but derive from three products/six pages, with old/undated and same-respondent material. Learning-study heterogeneity, subgroup and observational limits must travel into later choices. [Problem index](../../plan/v2/research-pain-points.md), [art/motion](../../plan/v2/research-art-motion.md).
