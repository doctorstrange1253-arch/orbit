## 1. Governance Structure

To ensure successful execution of the Orbit Master Plan, governance is structured around three key decision gates:

```
Gate 1: Architecture & Security Sign-Off (Milestone 1 complete)
    ↓
Gate 2: Monetization & Economics Review (Milestone 2 complete)
    ↓
Gate 3: Public Beta Gate (Milestone 4 complete)
```

## 3. Decision Rules

- **Unanimity required** for Gate 3 (Public Beta launch).
- Any high-severity risk (R-02, R-03, R-06) must be mitigated and verified by Senior Product Critic before passing corresponding gate.
- Plan amendments require written change request doc in `docs/plan/change-requests/`.
