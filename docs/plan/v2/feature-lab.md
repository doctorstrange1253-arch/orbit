## Scoring rubric - proposed, not measured facts

Nine weighted dimensions use 1-5 integers, higher is better. Intermediate scores 2/4 mean intermediate judgment. The score measures editorial priority under assumptions, not empirical effect size, development certainty or a founder approval.

| Dimension | Weight | Score 1 | Score 3 | Score 5 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Learner impact | 25 | Little educational/problem relevance | Plausible improvement with an explicit learning job | Direct major learning bottleneck addressed with a measurable outcome hypothesis |
| Retention quality | 15 | Engagement without durable value | Useful recurring job, validation needed | Repeated valuable practice/support without pressure, with retention and wellbeing counter-metrics |
| Mentor supply/quality | 15 | No supply-quality benefit | Reduces one production/support/matching friction | Strong credible demand/quality/fulfillment loop hypothesis |
| Revenue/margin | 10 | Cost/complexity with no credible value path | Plausible sustainable value, cost unknown | Clear nonexploitative value/cost mechanism; still not proven revenue uplift |
| Differentiation | 12 | Standard widget with a themed label | Distinct useful flow | Original concrete mechanism/signature that reinforces the flywheel |
| Feasibility | 10 | Major new systems/unknown operations | Several existing surfaces plus bounded new coordination | Narrow slice reusing corrected foundations with clear ownership |
| Safety/risk desirability | 7 | Severe unresolved exposure or moral hazard | Material mitigable issues needing gates | Low inherent risk with explicit privacy/abuse guardrails; not current compliance certification |
| Theme/coherence | 3 | Disconnected novelty | Fits the product's identities | Space/Multiverse metaphor clarifies the actual learning/creator job |
| Reuse | 3 | Competing subsystem or duplicate implementation | Reuses some contracts/components | Extends a canonical shared block without concealing prerequisite repairs |

Weights sum to 100. Learner value receives the largest weight; mentor quality and durable retention reinforce the marketplace; differentiation remains significant but cannot outweigh ethical failure. Feasibility/risk/reuse discourage small-team sprawl. Theme fit is not allowed to rescue an educationally weak idea.

### Ethics is a hard filter

- PASS: no inherent forbidden mechanic identified in the proposed slice; implementation, consent and legal checks are still required.
- REVIEW: uncertain money/minor/live-interaction/privacy assumptions. It cannot enter the minimum-shippable recommendation unless a concrete safe slice is re-evaluated; no implied policy approval.
- REJECT: pay-to-win/rating purchase, gambling/loot boxes, coercive streaks/FOMO, public minors by default, hidden charges or emotional dependence. A score of 100 cannot compensate.

A separate 1-5 ethics assessment may explain concern severity, but has zero compensating weight. The hard status/required conditions determine eligibility. Rejection is a planning recommendation, not deletion or final founder policy.

### Calculation and ranking

Unfiltered score on a 0-100 scale is:

`25 * (SUMPRODUCT(dimensionScores, weights) / SUM(weights) - 1)`

Eligibility is separately PASS or blocked/review/deferred. Default ranking considers only eligible canonical candidates. Ties use learner score, feasibility and stable idea ID; duplicates do not earn extra slots. The model must preserve raw score and exclusion reason, not hide excluded ideas or collapse unknown facts into zero silently. Final shortlist is a proposed portfolio, not an automatic top-25 vote.

## Feasibility and build-slice discipline
