## Counterevidence and excluded claims

- A speculative claim that 90% of sales go to bestsellers was challenged by replies; it is not adopted as a statistic.
- Advice about the number of courses needed for success conflicted across experiences; no universal minimum was established.
- Community descriptions of subscription-program admission and ranking were uncertain; they are not current official policy evidence.
- Free-coupon strategies received conflicting advice; efficacy and conversion causality were not established.
- Replies speculated about course settings, demand and ownership. No configuration or copyright defect was verified.
- Some replies described eventual success and satisfaction with teaching; these threads are not uniformly negative or evidence the platform necessarily causes failure.

## Inferences, not feature decisions

The opened reports distinguish demand uncertainty, production opportunity risk, feedback usefulness, ongoing maintenance, discovery entry and portfolio income concentration. They do not establish frequencies or a winning intervention. A founder can evaluate these jobs and constraints in C2, but not treat the posts as guarantees that a particular bonus, ranking rule, pledge or course-creation mechanic works.

No Feature Card, mission, currency value, revenue guarantee, creator policy or shortlist is finalized. Broader platform diversity, dated recent observations and Indian creator experience remain gaps; carry those limits into subsequent decisions instead of inventing evidence.
