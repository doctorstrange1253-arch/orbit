## Alternatives and recommendation

Build all 25 together maximizes coordination/load without proving any loop. Build only the highest numerical scores ignores dependencies and private exit/access rights. Build only spectacle risks a themed interface around broken learning/payments. Recommend a narrow vertical slice through corrected authority/version/entitlement/graded-source/receipt services, then evaluate additional connected artifacts under explicit mentor/privacy/support capacity. Exact pilot membership and later release waves remain the founder's decision.

The C2 score is an editorial priority model, not an effect size, delivery estimate or probability of success. Twenty proposed candidates survive all 22 weight scenarios, but simultaneous score errors, missing costs and source concentration are not tested by weight sweeps. In particular, coordinator-supplied mentor scores were not independently judged after the workflow stopped. Do not convert stable modeled ranks into final feature certainty.

## Five wow hypotheses and adversarial acceptance

- Outline Evidence Ballot is distinctive only if an attempt demonstrably improves the outline and closes a requester/mentor loop; its static evidence diff is the complete experience. Reject free-labor/earnings guarantees.
- Assumption Duet is distinctive only if two private paths reveal a useful missing bridge; a colored overlay alone is generic decoration. Reject shame, hidden disclosure, open pairing without staff or public errors.
- Learner Safe Course Refit is distinctive only if earlier reasoning/version context survives an authorized change; a new map animation cannot compensate for lost notes/rubrics or improper old-content retention.
- Revision Exhibit is distinctive only if “why I changed it” is useful; public sharing remains a separately approved extension. Reject artifact polish as an ability/rank proxy.
- Intent Fork is distinctive only if the chosen outcome visibly explains a reversible route; manual curated reasoning/undo must work in 2D. Reject opaque guaranteed matching and compulsory cinematic onboarding.

Each ordinary reveal/compare obeys the F8 optional/static/sound-off contract. No new renderer/AI model/wallet/social feed is needed solely to justify a wow label. Names/global originality/art/trademark clearance remain unverified and pending explicit founder branding choice.

## Capacity simulation: independence from preference scores

Use the C2 lore fixed assumption 224 content/review/art/ops hours/year. For a hypothetical operations budget of eight hours/week over 52 weeks, availability is 416 hours/year. Add a hypothetical two hours/week for support/triage (104/year) and eight minutes per accepted review task. These are deliberately synthetic workload inputs, not actual staff capacity or measured handling times; software/legal/incident/localization costs still excluded.

`annual load = 224 + 2*52 + weeklyTasks*8/60*52`; `capacity = 8*52`; `spare = capacity-load`.

| Synthetic accepted review tasks/week | Annual review hours | Total annual load | Spare hours (negative means overload) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 10 | 69.33 | 397.33 | 18.67 |
| 40 | 277.33 | 605.33 | -189.33 |
| 100 | 693.33 | 1,021.33 | -605.33 |

The illustration is to be checked numerically at document verification. At the smallest case, very little buffer remains for excluded incident/legal/software work. Growth in cooperation/review features can overload a stable-score portfolio without any weight change. Do not sell a public SLA, require unpaid mentor output, silently increase rewards, relax moderation or build live social scope to “solve” a capacity deficit.

Containment proposal: set an approved review-slot cap before intake, disclose waiting/unavailable state, default to private/manual/asynchronous slices, share one support/rights/Board/version pipeline, and skip optional live-ops when capacity is missing. No cap/SLA or staffing budget is finalized here. Fixed annual lore hours are themselves uncertain and should be replaced with measured pilot time.

## Portfolio counterfactuals and coherent scope options

- If foundation repairs are larger than assumed, keep private goal/return/plain-layout/source-backed support/earnings explanations and pause connected artifact/live-cooperation scope. Do not count mandatory fixes as new innovation.
- If reviewed question/pattern supply is weak, keep mentor-authored manual examples rather than ungrounded AI or duplicate copied tasks. Retention statistics cannot substitute for correct teaching material.
- If privacy/rights/safeguarding review is unavailable, keep X-012 private and defer X-042/049/083 public/live/minor extensions; a score of 70 or PASS does not grant policy readiness.
- If capacity is scarce, reconsider merging X-007/016/060 and borderline X-053/069 as functional extensions; C2 records remain intact until founder membership decisions.
- If real-session contribution turns negative under measured costs, do not change the fixed 15% share or raise price/make sessions compulsory without approval. Evaluate reliability/support/process costs and optional usage first; subscriptions remain separate.
- If meaningful motion fails device budgets, keep the complete static/2D workflow; reduce optional effects instead of hiding a low-end user from learning.

These are decision options/recommendations, not changes to the founder's three identities, core requested features, money constraints, approved app files or final portfolio.

## Evidence debt and validation order

First verify canonical authority/entitlements/ledger/Board/versioned grading with actual future tests. Then test task/copy/permission comprehension privately with authorized adults and appropriate review. Then measure delayed learning/repair outcomes and actual operator labor. Only afterward assess retention/liquidity/supply effects with clear denominators and ethics/consent/stop rules. No real-user experiment, paid service, survey, inference or provider call ran in D.
