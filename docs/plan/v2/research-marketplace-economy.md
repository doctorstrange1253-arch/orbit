**Rating evidence:** the 2009 Wilson explanation is explicitly binary. The 2012 article formalizes prior/time/risk judgments but also uses up/down votes. The 2014 article addresses star histograms using a Dirichlet prior (one pseudocount per category in its example) and an approximate posterior mean/variance lower bound. These are distinct tools; applying Wilson directly to a five-star mean or treating the author's illustrative loss multiple as an approved product value is unsupported. A prior strength, confidence level, recency rule and cold-start policy remain later design/tuning decisions.

**Notification study facts:** Yancey and Settles describe eligibility-dependent templates, novelty/recency, small-sample regularization and exploration. Their reward is lesson completion within two hours of a notification; they acknowledge that organic activity cannot be cleanly distinguished. Offline data comprise 34 days, with 88 million training and 114 million test decision rows, not that many people. Section 3.2 describes randomly assigned user groups, the existing uniform-template policy as control and two-week results: approximately +0.5% daily active users, +0.4% total lessons and about +2% new-user recurring retention. It optimizes content among eligible reminders, not whether to send versus not send, their total frequency, opt-out burden or wellbeing. Exact per-row table associations are not inferred from the flattened PDF table.

The paper's appendix describes logging eligibility/probabilities/selected template and a later random holdout for ongoing evaluation. These are evidence of a decision-evaluation pattern, not instructions to copy indefinite data retention into this platform. Company-authored academic work is more detailed than the product association articles, but is not independent replication or an India result.

**Inferences:** uncertain small samples and eligibility can distort rankings; sustained exploration needs an explicit evaluation/ethics boundary. Accurate event logs support analysis but do not confer reward authority. Engagement changes are not mastery gains. No allocator, model hyperparameter, notification cap or financial incentive is finalized here.

## C01 - Facts and limits

C01-S001 illustrates why a raw average can place a tiny sample above a much larger, consistently positive sample. It proposes the lower bound of a Wilson confidence interval for a Bernoulli proportion and explicitly limits its formula to positive/negative ratings. Locator: "WRONG SOLUTION #2" and "CORRECT SOLUTION". Short excerpt: "not a 5-star scale".

**Inference:** Applying the binary Wilson formula directly to an ordinary 1-5 mean is not supported. The later linked star-aware article provides a separate approximation, not a validated product decision. The 2009 article's informal probability wording must not be mistaken for a Bayesian posterior or evidence that its confidence setting is the right tuning choice here.

ME-S09 documents invitations that require mutual following and acceptance, up to five separate friend streaks, and reminders. That is evidence of an opt-in product mechanism, not a measured fatigue limit or a demand-allocation algorithm.

**Unknown:** wave sizes, claim windows, cold-start slots, reliability weighting, anti-collusion effects and any ranking winner. No Orbit weights or allocator are chosen.

## C02 - Facts, tension and transfer limits

| Source / locator | What is actually reported | What cannot be concluded |
|---|---|---|
| ME-S09, introductory result paragraph | Learners with at least one shared streak are reported as 22% more likely to complete a daily lesson | Assignment, comparator, sample, uncertainty, India effects and learning gains are not supplied |

| ME-S16, consistency/animation paragraph | Vendor reports +1.7% likelihood of a new learner still using the app seven days later after animation changes | Percentage points versus relative percent and study protocol are not resolved |

The worker's reading of ME-S16 also records the provider acknowledging that streak loss and rigid daily obligations can discourage participation, including during care/work/offline time. This is a provider acknowledgement, not measured prevalence. The coordinator independently checked the reported activity numbers and dates, not a separate underlying experiment.

**Inference:** Product activity/retention and learning outcomes are different measures. A vendor can report beneficial streak-associated activity while acknowledging pressure or inflexibility; those claims are not independent evidence against each other. Research still needs original SDT/autonomy work, independent adverse-effect evidence and current official league/seller-tier criteria.

**Opinion:** No progression recommendation is finalized from this limited evidence. Fogg, Hook and Octalysis remain framework names requested for investigation, not validated effects in this corpus.

## C04 - Facts, comparisons and limits

| Mechanism | Opened evidence | Guarantee boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Retry identity | ME-S06: persist the operation ID before submission and reuse it after network failures or restarts | Same-ID deduplication does not authorize the action or recognize equivalent awards sent under different IDs |
| Pending funds | ME-S03: reserve separately from posted balances; post, void or expire with invariant checks | Pending resolution cannot repeatedly or contradictorily finalize the same transfer |
| Additive correction | ME-S07: correcting a posted error adds reversing/adjusting transfers and retains history | This is not the same as voiding an unresolved reservation |
| Asset precision | ME-S08: integer amounts with application-defined scale and separate ledgers | Float approximation is avoided, but scale/ledger migration and business authorization still need design |
| Ledger identity | ME-S08: only same-ledger accounts transact directly; balances derive from debits/credits | Does not establish this product's currency conversion, tax or user-entitlement rules |

ME-S08 describes asset-scale changes as a different ledger/account migration because records are immutable. It is a vendor-specific documented constraint, not a current Orbit defect.

**Inference:** Accounting integrity and sustainable issuance are separate. A correct ledger can accurately record runaway rewards. The five TigerBeetle pages do not supply faucet/sink ratios, inflation targets, conversion policy, minors safeguards or independent performance comparisons.

**Opinion:** No database/vendor is selected. The current research supports checking operation identity, conservation, correction history and precision explicitly in the later model; it does not justify final economic values.
