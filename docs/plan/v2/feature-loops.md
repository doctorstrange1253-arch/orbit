# Feature Lab flywheel and humane retention loops

Audience: product, engineering and operations deciding how the additional features work together.

## One product, not three competing games

The intended flywheel is requests -> credible mentor outline/build -> student learning/feedback -> useful repair evidence -> mentor quality/reputation -> better demand and supply. F1 owns request allocation/claim lifecycle; F2 owns verified rating; F3/F4 own identity-specific leagues; F5 owns separate virtual ledgers; F6/F7 own authorized learning/call tools; F8 owns the common motion system. Extra features may provide evidence, never bypass those authorities.

[Portfolio and waves](feature-lab.md), [fourteen cards](feature-card-pack-01.md) and [source limitations](research-pain-points.md) form the contract. Loops below are hypotheses, not causal retention forecasts. Daily/weekly/seasonal means an available useful rhythm, not required attendance. No subscription/session incentive is created by these loops: optional sessions retain the separate 15% share, subscriptions the separate 20% share; neither virtual points nor support access imply cash entitlement.

## Behavior frameworks, used as critique rather than proof

- Fogg-style ability/prompt reasoning: reduce friction through preserved anchors, clear availability and plain layouts; the learner chooses the prompt. A notification cannot compensate for a broken workflow.
- SDT-style autonomy/competence/relatedness: optional review and correction, comprehensible evidence of progress, voluntary bounded cooperation. No claim that the framework proves these features work; direct evidence/operational debt remains in Phase C.
- Hook-style trigger/action/payoff/investment: use deterministic useful outcomes (resolved question, preserved reasoning), not variable monetary rewards or manufactured anxiety. Investments remain exportable/deletable; leaving never loses standing.
- Octalysis/game-design lens: creation, explanation, mastery and narrative make authentic work legible. Reject scarcity, social shame, random reward and completion compulsion. These are design heuristics, not measured learning effects.
- Retrieval/spacing/feedback/interleaving/metacognition: tie practice to versioned mentor rubrics and later transfer tasks; adjustability and heterogeneity matter. Private confidence is not a grade, engagement is not learning, and time spent is not an outcome.

## Shared attention and burnout proposal

1. Default extra-feature notifications are off until chosen. Transactional/safety messages remain under their separately approved policy; do not bundle them with promotional/live-ops prompts.
2. Per identity, combine chosen reminders into one configurable digest rather than competing per-feature pings. Proposed maximum one extra-feature digest/day and one event invitation/cycle, never a claim that those numbers are scientifically safe.
3. Quiet hours, snooze, per-feature disable and global disable are available. Revisited/closed/deleted work cancels obsolete reminders through idempotent workers. Time zones and delivery retries do not multiply prompts.
4. No “everyone is waiting,” loss countdown or public missed-event mark. Every live-ops artifact remains available asynchronously when rights/policy permit; attendance yields no rank/economic advantage.
5. A learner may choose zero recurring loops. Success includes a useful one-time task and a clean exit, not just repeated visits.

## Validation plan, not a running experiment

Before any later experiment: obtain approved scope/safety/privacy basis, define versions/cohorts, predefine outcomes/countermetrics and stopping criteria, confirm staffing and instrument access-controlled events. No A/B test, provider call or real-user survey was run in C2.

Proposed evaluation order: usability/comprehension and semantic-equivalence checks; then small consenting-adult pilots; then delayed rubric-scored learning/repair outcomes; only afterward retention/marketplace evaluation. Report denominators, exposure windows, nonresponse and uncertainty. Never infer causal effects from before/after anecdotes or use minors/live-money experiments as convenient first cohorts.

Stop/pause a pilot on a confirmed unauthorized disclosure, incorrect financial paid-status claim, compulsory participation/retaliation report requiring safeguarding review, or unbounded support/review workload. Exact incident thresholds, legal duties and response policy need competent approval; this is a launch checklist hypothesis, not finalized safety policy.
