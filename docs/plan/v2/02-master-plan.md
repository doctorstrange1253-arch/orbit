## 2. Product intent, alternatives and anti-goals

The intended job is to request an outcome, help define it, let a credible mentor build it, learn with reliable tools, repair mistakes privately, inspect meaningful evidence and generate the next useful demand. Retention and learning benefits are hypotheses to validate, not causal effects established by models or qualitative complaints.

| Approach | Trade-off | Proposed disposition |
|---|---|---|
| Separate feature engines/wallets/editors and visual-first release | Fast demos, duplicated authority and incompatible recovery/privacy | Reject as a delivery architecture |
| Replace the whole application and migrate legacy data immediately | Broad redesign and unproven compatibility; unnecessary scope | Reject without a separately approved migration case |
| Shared foundation plus small private vertical slices | Slower first authoritative slice, reusable source/grant/receipt/access contracts | Recommended proposal; not a founder-selected release scope |

Anti-goals: fabricated mastery or expertise; paid rank/reviews/claims; time, attendance, positive-review, bribery or grind rewards; loot/scarcity/pressure; identity collapse; compulsory public sharing; minor public ranking by default; permanent call retention; bought security/core learning/basic export/support; automatic AI publication/grades; model-driven bank-paid claims.

## 4. Flywheel and identity-specific boundaries

| Link | User action and durable truth | Useful feedback / explicit non-effect |
|---|---|---|
| Demand -> scope | Member states an outcome; approved scope/version and own interest | Interest is not enrollment, payment, forecast or consent to material scope expansion |
| Scope -> supply | Qualified available Mentor receives an actually accessible bounded offer | Delivery/acknowledgment/rank are separate; neither reserves a claim |
| Supply -> creation | Exclusive current claim and authoring grant; reviewed outline and real milestones | No ownership from a URL, elapsed-time percentage or automatic rights transfer |
| Creation -> learning | Processed, reviewed, access-controlled canonical publication | Local save/upload/provider acceptance alone does not publish |
| Learning -> repair | Registered grade evidence and private source-linked notes/mistakes | Notes, stars, watching and model suggestions are not verified mastery |
| Repair -> recognition | Approved comparable improvement or reviewed original contribution | Only permitted source families; no repeat or cross-identity issuance by presentation switch |
| Evidence -> next demand | Safe scoped outcomes/reviews and unmet outcome proposals | No private learner drill-down, causal effect inference or income guarantee |

Student loop: own learning, feedback, chosen review and optional comparable progress. Mentor loop: credible demand, bounded creation/repair, reviewed contributions and transparent obligations. Peer loop: authorized purposeful exchange and shared ephemeral tools, not raw chat/call-ended minting. Optional cadence is user-chosen and policy-bounded; no daily attendance debt or loss-pressure live-ops.

## 7. Privacy, consent and purpose states

Learner notes, mistake reflections and error artifacts are owner-private. Mentor course ownership, room membership and a grading appointment do not grant those notes. Public/minor/sparse-cohort branches remain off unless their exact policy is approved. Previously valid receipts are reauthorized/redacted at lookup; historic permission cannot expose current private fields.

Recording is optional and off by default. New recording requires every required participant's current purpose/scope choice plus independently valid booking/participant/age/guardian/safety/retention authority. A change of purpose/visibility/retention/scope requires explicit new choices. Payment, role, camera-on or silence is not consent.

`consent.recording.withdraw` is deny-only: authenticate account and authorize ownership of that participant's consent scope; commit a revocation fence under approved minimal security/retention authority even if recording-use policy is suspended. Do not require Mentor role or all-party re-consent. If durable authority is unavailable, local capture still stops immediately, new recording/renewal/ingest fails closed and durable/remote acknowledgment is explicitly unavailable. An independently eligible nonrecording call may continue; unknown mandatory guardian/session eligibility blocks that affected session, never the local stop control. Prior authorized bytes remain subject to approved retention/deletion/hold policy, not promised instant erasure.

EPHEMERAL_CALL preserves deliberate call-end/expiry semantics. PERSISTENT_STUDIO and COURSE_PUBLISHED have distinct rights, retention, parent access and grants. A changed URL/mode cannot retain a call Board or publish/export another person's media. Private offline persistence stays off until shared-device disclosure, encryption/key lifecycle, logout disposal and retention are approved/tested.
