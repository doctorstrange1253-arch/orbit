## What this count means

**33 distinct coded problem observations** are retained after merging four overlapping themes from 37 raw experience observations: eight coordinator learner observations, 15 LE observations and 14 recovered CE observations. The additional creator help request is not counted as a fault. There are 22 learner-oriented and 11 creator-oriented entries.

This is not 33 people, incidents, representative complaints or confirmed current vendor defects. It is a traceable qualitative problem corpus across three products (Coursera, Duolingo, Udemy), six opened public source pages/feeds, several old reviews and two creator threads with unknown publication dates. Several distinct problems originate in the same review/post. No prevalence, ranking of frequency, causal effect, vendor intent or commercial revenue claim follows from it.

Verification means the report text was read in the cited source. Underlying functionality, disputed grades, earnings and algorithm claims were not independently reproduced or audited. US-store selection does not establish residence. The coordinator feed deliberately selected low-rated entries; the other lists are convenience samples. Negative entries and positive counterexamples must both be retained.

## Deduplication audit

37 raw experience observations become 33 coded problems through four merges: C12-P001 with LE-P10 (support); CE-R01 with CE-R06 (production/portfolio effort); CE-R02 with CE-R14 (paid conversion); CE-R07 with CE-R09 (catalog-income uncertainty). CE-N01 is retained as an expressed need in the creator note but excluded from the raw 37 and the 33 count. Technical, legal-policy and vendor-documentation constraints elsewhere are not added as user complaints.

Distinct problems from the same respondent remain different jobs, but not independent corroboration. Other overlap remains for analysis (for example progress loss versus paid assessment access); those concrete states are not collapsed into an assertion about defect prevalence.

## Counterevidence and coverage gaps

Many reviewers and creators also praise learning content, later feedback, eventual success or teaching satisfaction. Some old app defects were reported fixed, and autoplay accounts conflict across dates. Keep these qualifications when generating later ideas.

The numerical minimum is met for coded real source reports, but coverage is concentrated: no substantive Unacademy, Topmate, Preply, Teachable or Skillshare complaint sample, representative Android cohort, Indian prevalence, guardian/institution interview or actual current product test is established. This corpus does not rank the most important problems or prove what intervention will solve them. Expand/redirect evidence if those identities/platforms become scope-critical.

No 60-idea list, scoring weights, Feature Card, league/currency name, new money flow or scope choice is produced here. Those are gated Phase C2 and later decisions.
