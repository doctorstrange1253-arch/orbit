Companion to [the existing-system audit](01-existing-system-audit.md). This preserves 110 returned-report records plus one coordinator documentation finding. Records include duplicates, gaps and rejected hypotheses; this is NOT a count of 111 proven bugs.

## Verification labels

- V: an independent verifier returned a source-checked finding. No runtime reproduction.
- T: coordinator performed a targeted source/guard check after the workflow was stopped. No runtime reproduction.
- A: first-pass audit finding only; independent verification was stopped. UNVERIFIED for confirmation; do not treat as an established defect or ship a fix without checking it.
- U: explicitly plausible/unverified hypothesis, including browser-dependent behavior.
- G: future requirement gap or policy difference, not automatically a bug.
- R: rejected hypothesis or deliberate current behavior; retained to prevent repeated false alarms.

FIN/MED records use completed verification reports. REP/EXP second-pass workers were stopped at the user-selected time/coverage tradeoff. Targeted checks do not retroactively complete those workers. Aliases identify duplicate underlying findings, not extra independent evidence. AI-02 is dormant even though source-checked.

Record-label totals (including aliases): V=53, G=6, R=10, T=13, A=27, U=2. Six alias records are explicit below.

Priorities are proposed triage labels from source inspection, not evidence of exploitation or current production configuration. All application tests, browser actions, provider calls and database access were excluded.
