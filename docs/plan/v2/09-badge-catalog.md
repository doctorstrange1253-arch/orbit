## 1. Purpose, evidence and non-goals

Recognize deterministic, rights-permitted teaching craft with private inspectable evidence. A badge is an achievement record and optional original visual keepsake, not an expertise certificate, public rank, claim grant, money asset or purchased advantage.

Read dependencies are the written [shared platform contracts](03-platform-contracts.md), [Mentor Playground](06-mentor-playground.md), [Student Playground](07-student-playground.md), [D playground reasoning](thinking-playgrounds.md), [humane loops](feature-loops.md), and [lore](../../design/v2/lore-live-ops.md). Predicate definitions and producer/schema/event/index mappings are owned by [mission catalogs](08-mission-catalogs.md), returned with this document.

Only those six named planning/design files were successfully read; an initial outer-path contract read failed before the nested written file was located and read first. Existing art/tokens/Motion/Three/Board/notes reuse claims are inherited from the plans, not newly source-audited.

Inventory: M-BDG-001 through M-BDG-040. Every name, criterion, SVG recipe, window, permission and release choice is PROPOSED. Forty authored recipe proposals do not mean forty generated, rendered, shipped, licensed or trademark-cleared assets.

### Approaches considered

| Approach | Tradeoff | Disposition |
|---|---|---|
| Stock trophy/star recolors and volume tiers | Cheap, but repeated symbolism and rewards quantity/status rather than distinct craft | Rejected |

No badge for positive reviews, follower counts, messages, attendance, time online, payment, course price, top-rank status, recruiting minors, recording consent or refusing a refund. No random drops, scarcity, expiration pressure, cash-out, fee reduction, grade/rating/rank boost or claim priority.

Only known F1-F9 scope is covered. Missing original detailed E/F instructions are not recovered. MAIN alone writes/registers/logs returned files; no application or asset files are created here.

## 2. Shared badge authority and criterion semantics

1. Badge identity is Mentor, authenticated through server-only ActorContext with current account identity, resource ACL and live sessionGeneration/authzEpoch/membership/consent fences as applicable.
2. Accepted source evidence comes from the exact registered schemas in 08: ML lesson, MO outcome, MK milestone, MQ resolution and MP pattern. Consumers cannot accept browser flags, helper/model output or socket payloads as proof.
3. A source must have accepted provenance, sourceVersion/evidenceVersion/rubricVersion and the original authorized F3 source decision. Cap-clipped useful evidence is eligible only under approved badge policy.
4. Catalog criteria are evaluated against underlying accepted evidence, never a mutable mission UI status, badge count, current league tier or today's reweighted score.
5. All source counts below mean distinct authoritative equivalence families, not files, names, operation IDs, policies, mission labels or copies. Unknown lineage blocks award.
6. Badge achievement uses shared benefit family badge.achievement only after explicit source-registry approval. Default F3 mentor.quality sources do not automatically authorize a second benefit.
7. Proposed derived badge source is bound to owner/identity/immutable stableBadgeFamilyId and the complete criterion evidence set. That family represents one lifetime achievement, not a new publication/outcome reward.
8. The same badge family can be awarded once lifetime across catalog/policy/art/name changes. Revisions and renewed evidence update linked status/history, never mint a second badge or reopen its semantic basis.
9. Multiple different badges using overlapping inputs require explicit approved derived-benefit mapping. Each distinct immutable criterion has a separate badge-achievement family; a local handler cannot invent one to evade consumption.
10. SourceRegistry/SourceConsumption, badge domain/invariant projection, receipt and outbox commit atomically. Badge records do not add points or ledger entries; no fake asset leg represents a badge.
11. Original F3 source cycles remain fixed. Composite evidence may span cycles; its achievement date is the authoritative recognition commit, not permission to relocate original sources or points.
12. Correction/fraud/rights decisions are linked, specific and appealable. No accusation badge, wholesale deletion, silent old-receipt rewrite, negative virtual balance or financial clawback is allowed.
13. Owner-private Student answers/artifacts/notes remain inaccessible to Mentors merely because they own a course. MO uses only approved minimal outcome findings, not raw private learning evidence.
14. All badges default private. Eligible adult opt-in external sharing requires separate approved audience/rights/safety policy and field allowlist. Minors are not publicly ranked by default; unknown eligibility never enables public sharing/ranking.
15. Rest/opt-out does not expire earned art or create attendance debt. Evidence windows below are optional evaluation scopes, not required schedules or consecutive-participation tests.

Mentor rates/caps remain F3 proposals: lesson30/cap120, outcome40/cap160, milestone20/cap80, resolution10/cap60, pattern10/cap40. Category caps sum460; global cap400. Four-week weights1/.7/.49/.343 over2.533 affect current standing only, not lifetime badge possession.

Additional badge points/virtual units/INR are all zero. Badges cannot convert Student/Mentor/Peer virtual assets or cash. Fixed session15% and separate subscription20%/mentor-pool80% remain unchanged; bases/fees/tax/refund/reserve/KYC/allocation are blocked qualified-policy decisions.

## 3. Criterion notation, windows and protection profiles

B is all applicable section 2 authority/provenance/original-receipt/policy checks. LP01-LP06, OR01-OR06, KM01-KM06, QR01-QR06 and PT01-PT06 have exactly the definitions in 08; no duplicated or modified meanings are introduced here.

`has(X)` means at least one currently accepted authoritative source with registered check X=true. `same(X,Y)` means one source has both approved findings; it still counts once as its original contribution. `distinct(X,Y)` requires different reviewed equivalence families. Every composite must have an approved cross-source relationship check, not a client assertion of linkage.

Window L: lifetime eligible accepted evidence available under approved retention; no deadline/expiry. Window P: the explicitly named canonical claim/repair/project lineage, regardless of how many weeks it spans; no attendance requirement. Window L excludes any future arbitrary clock cutoff.

For KM criteria, the accepted source includes original deadline/approved compliant excuse classification from F1. A late review is not lateness by the Mentor. Time spent or attending a meeting never satisfies a KM badge.

| Protection profile | Evidence/access conditions | Revocation, appeal and fraud rule |
|---|---|---|
| C | ML, own rights-permitted teaching artifact plus appointed content/rights review; accessible text/static equivalent accepted | Invalidate only the implicated lesson finding; independently appointed appeal; split/copy/reupload never adds a family |
| O | MO, approved minimal repair/outcome findings; learner details suppressed and individually scoped | Review specific outcome/rights error; no causal claim or raw learner drill-down; appointed nonconflicted appeal |
| K | MK, F1 accepted claim/milestone source, original scope/version/deadline and rights | Specific claim-source correction; no retroactive deadline edit, milestone splitting, attendance reward or punitive disability inference |
| Q | MQ, current question-purpose grant and appointed resolution finding | Specific resolution correction; no likes/stars/message-count proof, compelled praise or self-approval; private question stays private |
| P | MP, reviewed attribution/rights and independent reusable teaching pattern | Specific rights/fraud finding; provenance retained across forks; fingerprint is not perfect copying detection; appeal available |
| X | Composite, intersection of every referenced profile and explicit relationship evidence | Recompute only from eligible distinct families/accepted relationship; no threshold laundering through mission aliases, copied files or stale indexes |

Every catalog row inherits its profile's eligibility/privacy/accessibility/revocation/appeal/fraud conditions plus B. No row permits an award under unknown/suspended required policy. Private support remains usable regardless of badge status.

## 9. Atomic award and correction algorithm

1. Validate envelope/allowlist, authenticate server context and authorize progression/evidence-set discovery before any source detail.
2. Resolve actor/operation idempotency. Exact retry returns the original permitted receipt; changed intent returns OPERATION_CONFLICT. Reauthorization applies to historical receipt fields.
3. Inside the shared transaction resolve approved badge policy, immutable definition, registered underlying source versions, original receipts and accepted relationship evidence.
4. Validate all source ACLs, rights, lineage, distinct-family counts and criterion checks. A stale projection/mission label never supplies proof. Unknown source/reviewer/normalization/retention blocks the affected branch.
5. Touch live subject/resource/membership/consent fences and CAS progression/award versions. Current role loss blocks new Mentor achievement; independent case rights and financial liabilities remain unaffected.
6. Resolve the preapproved stable derived achievement source and shared badge.achievement basis. An existing award/basis returns no_change or approved linked status update, never another achievement.
7. Commit derived source acceptance/consumption, award/history, invariant projection, receipt, operation completion and outbox together through the shared coordinator. ledgerPlans is empty; original teaching source/caps are unchanged.
8. Return durable receipt. Optional read projection/notification/art display is asynchronous and recoverable; channel acceptance does not mean the owner saw the badge.
9. On a corrected source, reverse-index affected evidence sets, revalidate complete criterion and policy, and stage a specific review_pending/revoked/remains-earned decision. A replacement already-accepted evidence set can preserve eligibility only if approved correction policy permits it; do not silently swap proof.
10. Appeal can uphold, revise or reinstate the existing award with linked history. Never reset basisKey, awardedAt history, original receipt, lifetime source consumption or whole-user history.

| Failure boundary | Expected durable truth |
|---|---|
| Crash before/during source/award/projection/receipt/outbox commit | All business effects absent, or complete together; no half-earned badge |
| Commit succeeds, HTTP response lost | Original receipt resolves same operation; no second source, award or ceremony |
| Award committed, art file/notification unavailable | Evidence award stays committed; neutral text/fallback and honest delivery/art state |
| Share withdrawal races with read | Live fence ordering; later unauthorized reads/share grants fail, cache clears; delivered bytes cannot be recalled |
| Registry/retention/atomic authority unavailable | POLICY_BLOCKED/TEMPORARILY_UNAVAILABLE; no invented source or success |
