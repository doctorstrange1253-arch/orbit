## 4. Learner-local80% pool - candidate methods and exact synthetic vectors

Sources: [CFT-I04](research-course-tiers-subject-routing.md#cft-i04), [CFT-I14](research-course-tiers-subject-routing.md#cft-i14). Figures below supplied synthetic arithmetic hain, actual prices, costs, settled payments or founder-selected rates nahi.

Let `B_l` approved recognized basis in integer INR paise ho; gross checkout/net-of-tax meaning assumed nahi. Candidate HALF_UP platform split `P_l=floor((20*B_l+50)/100)` aur `H_l=B_l-P_l` hai.20% fixed hai; paise rounding itself unapproved hai. `E_l` THAT learner ka eligible course/version/sole-beneficiary snapshot ho; co-owner or unresolved ownership branch blocked hai until its beneficiary map is approved.

Positive-denominator candidate: Mentor m ka pre-rounding amount `H_l * sum(u_c for c in E_l owned by m) / sum(u_c for c in E_l)`.
- Equal-course: every eligible distinct course `u_c=1`. Mentor amounts course rows sum karke bante hain.
- Reviewed units: `u_c` separate approved positive integer decision se; class, displayed price, tags, duration, ratings or league alone se nahi.
- Equal-unique-Mentor different method hai: one weight per eligible recipient. It cannot be silently substituted for a course/unit allocator.

**Proposed exact initial apportionment:** `W=sum(u_c)`; each row gets `q_c=floor(H_l*u_c/W)`, remainder `r_c=(H_l*u_c) mod W`. Remaining `H_l-sum(q_c)` paise descending remainders mein; ties immutable server-issued course IDs ki one approved bytewise order se. Course rows first, frozen Mentor aggregation afterward. Approved positive integer bounds/checked arithmetic required; repeated legitimate numeric unit values allowed hain.

## 5. Fake-expensive claims - evidence, review and appeal

**Proposed distinction:** Falsifiable misrepresentation aur subjective-value dissatisfaction separate tracks hon. High price alone fraud verdict nahi; legal fraud classification qualified-review matter hai. Advertised included deliverables, access period, resource rights, credentials, preview/syllabus, support scope aur factual reference-price/discount claims reviewable hain. Learner ki not-worth-it feedback allowed rahe, but it alone does not establish factual falsity.

### 5.1 Qualitative rubric, not invented numerical scoring

This author/returned-Q proposal is not a retrieved formal course-design standard. Each dimension has `Pass / Needs revision / Unknown`, reason and bounded evidence reference; numerical weights/cutoffs require calibration.

| Dimension | Reviewer checks |
|---|---|
| Audience/outcomes |Clear intended learner/prerequisites; assessable outcomes; taught outcomes vs assumed prerequisites. |
| Instructional alignment |Outcome-to-lesson/activity/resource map; honest preview/syllabus; useful examples/practice; unsupported promises identified. |
| Usability/accessibility |Usable media/resources and approved accessible alternatives; quality targets are not measured device/learning guarantees. |
| Rights/authorship/credentials |Allowed course assets/licenses/authorship and precise credential claims; no plagiarism detector or job/licensure guarantee. |
| Included delivery/support |Exact scope/channel/period/capacity/substitutes; separate optional paid sessions; no vague unlimited commitment. |
| Cost/class rationale |Supported commercial delivery/maintenance profile against a versioned class rubric; self-price, tags, duration, followers and stars are not approval. |
| Versions/change disclosure |Manifest, class decision, obligations and review evidence versions; stale promises/update claims reviewed. |

Baseline applies to all classes. T3 is not an escape hatch for unknown rights, misleading claims or absent review. Review can verify stated evidence, not universal intrinsic worth, teaching effectiveness or guaranteed future delivery.

### 5.2 Authority and affordable capacity

Mentor submits factual manifest and proposed tags/class; appointed subject-relevant independent reviewer decides. Reviewer cannot be author, co-owner, beneficiary, paid promoter or conflicted party; disclosure/recusal required. Member league rank does not establish reviewer competence. Appeal goes to a different reviewer with the disputed claims/evidence and a Mentor response opportunity.

Proposed immutable lifecycle: Draft -> Submitted -> Needs revision or Reviewed decision; appeal/correction creates a new linked decision; old decisions become Superseded rather than silently edited. Approved/suspended applicability is separately versioned. Record reviewer/assignment, conflict declaration, rubric version, evidence refs, reason, decision time and effective scope.

Capacity affordability must include intake, content/rights checks, source verification, disagreement, appeals, re-review, privacy/safety and support handling. Measure actual handling effort and included-delivery capacity in a bounded evaluation; no sample size, reviewer count, SLA, budget or automation accuracy is invented. Funding review from the learner80% pool or charging for approval is not silently authorized.

If appointed independent review/appeal capacity is missing, pause new classification/upgrade intake or use the curated/deferred alternative. A paid class cannot buy review/claim queue priority; safety triage remains separately approved. Existing obligations cannot disappear because the new queue is unavailable. No automatic T3 publication, indefinite money hold or assumed free staff substitute.

### 5.3 Abuse/accident cases and proportionate handling

| Scenario | Proposed guard; what must NOT happen |
|---|---|
| Cheap course labelled expensive with self-tags |Tags only proposal/triage evidence; authorized versioned class decision and separate unit decision required. No self-upgrade payout/rank. |
| Duration padding |Review outcomes, content structure/exercises and filler signals; no runtime-length/value shortcut. Legitimate practice/repetition/accessibility pacing not automatically rejected. |
| Split/duplicate/translation/sequel |Declare commercial family/lineage, reused assets/outcomes and distinct purpose; review legitimate derivatives vs count inflation. No automatic extra units or historical clawback. |
| Self-review/co-owner/reciprocal ratings |Not independent certification. Coordinated/repeated feedback signals investigation, not guilt; enrollment alone doesn't prove independence. |
| Shared device/network or complaint burst |Not collusion proof; no automatic ban, grade/rank penalty, debt or publicity. |
| Expired/unmet included support |Claim-specific re-review for new enrollment/upgrade; preserve accepted terms and create scoped delivery/remedy case. SESSION15% stays separate. |
| Post-enrollment upgrade/content/owner change |New reviewed snapshot with explicit prospective effect; no silent old access/beneficiary/obligation/payout rewrite. |
| Rapid slot switching |Approved selection cutoff/switch policy and frozen money cycle; no replay/double pool or presumed mid-cycle proration. |
| False or unsuccessful accusation |Report disputed promise/version/expected-vs-delivered and optional evidence. Good-faith unsupported report not punished; deliberate fabrication requires separately evidenced adjudication. |
| Rights/safety restriction |Current approved rights/safety authority may restrict delivery; immutable snapshot does not guarantee unsafe/unlicensed content forever. Money remedy remains separate. |

Reports and evidence are case-/purpose-scoped. Reviewer access to a course doesn't grant learner notebooks, answers, recordings or KYC/bank browsing. Notice, redaction, retention, appeal timing and remedy policy remain qualified/founder gates. Accessible report/withdraw/support paths need no paid rank or token.

Formal originals for course-quality review/appeal remain absent: [CFT-Q07](research-course-tiers-subject-routing.md#cft-q07), [CFT-Q08](research-course-tiers-subject-routing.md#cft-q08) blocked; [CFT-Q09](research-course-tiers-subject-routing.md#cft-q09), [CFT-Q10](research-course-tiers-subject-routing.md#cft-q10) are actual discovered but unopened leads, not current standards.
