**Verification meaning:** the reports existed in the opened feed. The underlying vendor faults or disputed grades were NOT independently tested. These are reported experiences, not confirmed product incidents. Eight entries are not necessarily eight different people.

## Scientific findings and transfer limits

| Evidence | What is reported | Interpretation limit |
|---|---|---|
| LE-S06 systematic-review conference abstract | 29 articles, 47 experiments and 75 effect sizes; retrieval/interleaving benefits relatively consistent, distributed practice less consistent in its school scope | Effect estimates, risk of bias and full methods unavailable; not a universal timing prescription |
| LE-S07 secondary synthesis | Spacing and interleaving differ; initial practice can feel slower while longer-term retention/discrimination improves | Does not provide this product's optimum schedule, difficulty normalization or causal retention effect |
| LE-S11 peer-reviewed meta-analysis | 435 studies, 994 effects and over 61,000 participants; abstract reports d=0.48; results give a raw estimate d=0.55 and 17% negative effects | Raw and abstract estimates are not interchangeable; 17% refers to effects, not learners; substantial heterogeneity and information-content moderators |
| LE-S12 primary experiment | Harder retrieval improves initial delayed recall, but immediate feedback at that delayed test reverses subsequent ordering | A boundary condition, not refutation of retrieval practice or proof harder is always better |
| LE-S13 primary experiment | Effort/luck/random/no-reward conditions; overall retest main effects not significant, some memory benefits limited to women/specific errors; engagement reports did not imply equal memory gains | Subgroup/task constraints and exclusions; no gender-specific product rule or universal reward conclusion |
| LE-S14 observational analysis | 569,311 PISA students and 20,597 schools; negative direct feedback/performance association with positive modeled indirect paths through self-concept/enjoyment | Cross-sectional mediation is not causation; 2018 data is not made current by 2026 publication; abstract/Methods geography counts differ |

**Inferences:** immediate success, engagement, retention and learning are different outcomes. Feedback quality and task conditions matter more than a blanket claim that more feedback or difficulty is better. A punitive score/league intervention is not validated by these studies. Self-reported enjoyment and actual delayed recall must be measured separately.

**Opinions/proposals, not selection:** require outcome and wellbeing counter-metrics when later evaluating missions/ratings/leagues. Do not treat course completion, streak length or clicks as verified mastery. No mission, scoring weights, reward values, learning schedule or Feature Card is finalized here.
