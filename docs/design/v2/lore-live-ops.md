# Proposed lore and live-ops bible - Continuity Observatory

Date: 2026-11-07. Task: FP2-C2. Status: PROPOSAL ONLY; no visible branding, feature scope, event cadence or safety policy approved.
Audience: the founder, future product/art engineers and the operations team.
[Skill: brainstorming]

## 1. Purpose and choice of direction

The narrative makes real learning/teaching actions legible: an unanswered question becomes a route; an assumption becomes a testable lens; a revision preserves its history. It is not a cosmetic franchise skin or a reason to keep learners online. Working title **Continuity Observatory** is a coordinator proposal, not a trademark search result, cleared brand or renaming of Orbit, leagues, badges or currencies.

Considered directions: (A) observatory of changing knowledge, which clarifies evidence/versioned reasoning; (B) academy of craft, which makes apprenticeship clear but risks prestige/ability hierarchy; (C) traveling archive, which emphasizes preservation but weakens the demand/creator loop. Recommend A with craft/archive activities inside it. This is not a founder choice. F3's three full league/badge naming/art directions and F4/F5 branding remain later core-feature work; this bible does not finalize them.

Extend [existing observatory/academy art principles](../00-art-direction.md) and [existing tokens](../01-design-tokens.md). Do not copy protected fiction's characters, logos, spacecraft silhouettes, terminology, music or story beats. The historical art document's reference works are not asset licenses. Use original authored vector constructions and diagrams whose meaning survives a flat, static interface. No new palette, renderer, icon font or UI framework is introduced by this proposal.

## 2. Canon, roles and vocabulary

**Canon premise.** An observatory charts explanations, not territory. Every useful map includes uncertainty, source and revision history. A changed understanding adds a layer rather than erasing an earlier explorer's work. “Multiverse” means multiple legitimate contexts/approaches; it is not a claim of unlimited licensed worlds or alternative truth in assessments.

**Role metaphors, optional and nonhierarchical.** Student is the observer who tests a route; mentor is the builder who makes a bridge understandable; Peer is the linker who helps two explanations meet. These are narrative descriptions, not account-role names or earned certification. Institutions/guardians are not invented characters; any future interface remains plain-purpose and access-scoped.

**Language rules.** Plain labels first; metaphor second and removable. Say “You changed your explanation,” “This source is unavailable,” “Choose whether to revisit,” “No recap is available for this group,” “This amount is not yet confirmed.” Do not say “Your crew needs you,” “Do not lose your place in history,” “Guaranteed earnings,” “A weak observer,” or “Only today's learners deserve this.” Support, consent, payments and safety copy stays literal.

**Artifact rules.** A revision map, source/uncertainty card or pattern can become a useful private keepsake. No rarity, randomized drops, sold completion, attendance badge, loot box or expiration pressure. Any eventual badge/collection reward must use independently approved F3-F5 criteria/economics; this bible adds no badge/currency names or faucets. User-generated layers remain permissioned; no public minors by default.

## 3. Narrative structure without coercive live service

A cycle has four optional beats: ask a useful question; compare a meaningful assumption; make/review a change; choose the next route or leave. No mandatory sequence, unlock gate or timer. Each activity has a full asynchronous equivalent, selected content remains available subject to rights/retention policy, and prior events do not require catch-up.

Crews, if later approved, are bounded exercise groups rather than public persistent social rankings. Minimum live-ops uses no live calls or open matching. Do not launch a “community” without staff/moderation/appeal capacity. Opt-out removes event prompts, not learning/support access. Adult cooperation pilots do not establish child safeguarding readiness.

**State machine proposal:** draft -> content/rights/access review -> approved-for-invitation -> optional active exercise -> reviewed artifact -> archived or withdrawn. Paused, suppressed, unavailable and revoked are visible states. No event may substitute for canonical course publication, claim ownership, verified ratings, cash release or account identity.

**Publishing checklist:** approved feature gate; approved content owner; exact role/access scope; rights/source review; honest functional instructions; accessible/static equivalent; privacy/withdrawal/export test; staff capacity; optional notification preview; no unapproved reward or pricing; incident owner and cancellation plan. “Approved-for-invitation” here is a future operational state, not permission granted in this document.

**Fixed annual assumption:** content 82 h + review 62 h + art 30 h + operations 50 h = 224 h. These hours exclude software work, core F1-F8 design, legal/accounting review, localization, user support, incident response and participant-level moderation. Do not treat 224 h as a budget commitment or a complete annual cost.

**Scalable workload model:** additional hours/month = `accepted review tasks * average reviewed minutes / 60 + support cases * average handling minutes / 60 + moderation cases * average handling minutes / 60 + incident/appeal hours`. All volumes/minutes are unknown inputs until piloted; there is no invented demand forecast. Proposed capacity policy: operations sets an explicit review-slot cap before invitation; overflow is unavailable/waiting by choice, not a hidden unpaid commitment. Reserve an explicitly staffed incident/appeal owner or cancel social participation.

## 5. Live-ops operating recipe

1. Four weeks before a proposed cycle, owner selects one already-approved feature job and a reusable content template. This is a planning lead time, not a software delivery promise.
2. Content author supplies task/example/rubric, rights/source register and plain instructions; reviewer checks fairness, correctness, accidental personal data and accessible equivalent.
3. Operations confirms slot capacity, issue/report route and cancellation authority. No unrestricted UGC invitation without moderation staff. Review throughput is measured in the pilot before a broader invitation.
4. Release only to the authorized audience who opted in. Proposed one invitation/cycle; optional digest follows [shared attention rules](../../plan/v2/feature-loops.md). Never use personal error histories or earnings to pressure attendance.
5. During activity, monitor access/rights incidents, harmful interaction, review backlog and semantic fidelity. Suppress/withdraw content or pause intake rather than hide failures or increase rank rewards.
6. Closure publishes only reviewed permissioned artifacts. Do not disclose attendance, individual struggles or group rank. Record what worked, unknowns and actual staff time; archived content does not imply indefinite rights/access.
7. Reuse useful tasks next cycle with clear versioning. Participants do not lose prior standing or need to finish a season. Annual recap is opt-in private, not an emotional dependency or public leaderboard.

## 6. Art and motion acceptance

Use original bespoke SVG geometry for bridges, lenses, pages, anchors and plates. Each construct has a functional label; never a Unicode pictograph icon. Keep existing token sources and shared card/Board/typography patterns; no artwork is generated or approved in C2. Clear the working titles/marks and any commissioned art/license before visible branding.

Semantic state is readable with no animation. Ordinary reveals may use the shared 180 ms/400 ms proposal from the [cards](../../plan/v2/feature-card-pack-01.md), sound off, instant reduced-motion state and a skip control. No flashes, unskippable ceremonies, costly full-screen particles or mandatory Three canvas. Device budgets/60 fps are future measured acceptance work, not established by this bible.

Check small screens, keyboard/screen reader, language expansion, no-color comparison, low bandwidth, low-power/static mode, revoked source and fully disabled live-ops. Any illustration that obscures amount/status/consent must be removed rather than treated as necessary theme.

## 7. Decisions and research debt

Founder must approve any working names/art direction, extra-feature membership, cadence and release scope separately. Safety/privacy owners must approve adult/child scopes, retention/incident rules, consent/suppression and moderator capacity. Finance/legal review must resolve money/applicability questions before core F5/F7 finalization; this lore adds no financial rule.

Unknowns: trademark/asset clearance, local-language interpretation, institutional/guardian demand, actual content effort, willingness to join, learning/retention effect, India minor/live moderation and media/vendor terms. A memorable metaphor is a design hypothesis. If functional utility is weak, keep the real tool and drop the lore rather than intensify engagement pressure.
