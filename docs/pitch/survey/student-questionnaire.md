## Before building the form

Proposed population: consenting college/university students aged18+. This is a research eligibility choice, not a statement of statutory age requirements or a selected launch market.
Fill the study owner, purpose, study-contact route, retention period and privacy notice. Use an external information page; do not save contact details in the repository.
Verify form-provider logging and metadata before promising anonymity. Disable automatic email collection, compulsory sign-in and unnecessary identifiers where supported. No names, exact colleges, handles or addresses.
Eligibility/consent are required; other questions may be skipped. For ordered scales retain order; randomize unordered choices where supported. “None” is exclusive in multi-select fields.
Core S01–S18 aims at under five minutes only after a measured pilot. Matrix fields still count as multiple questions. If too long, move S13/S16 to optional modules without silently pooling different instruments. Full optional questionnaire is not claimed to take five minutes.

## Neutral concept test — three sentences

A learning platform is being developed to connect students and mentors around courses learners can request or support with a vote. Its intended experience includes video lessons as levels, quizzes, gamified progress and optional mentor-priced one-to-one sessions. It is a prototype with partial and planned elements; not all features are built and no improvement in learning or completion has been established.

S18: Based only on this description, how likely are you to try it if suitable courses become available? Single numeric scale0–10, anchors “not at all likely”/“extremely likely”, plus cannot judge. Not a signup or purchase.

## Optional modules

S19 feature interest: separate1–5fields for requesting courses, supporting requests, lessons as levels, quizzes, gamified progress, leagues/missions, peer help and optional paid one-to-one sessions. Anchors not interested/slightly/moderately/very/extremely interested; cannot judge for each; separate exclusive none-of-these choice. Do not interpret interest as verified usefulness.

Price-module gate: show one consistent hypothetical offer card [FILL: course allowance/content/support]. No target prices as anchors. Exclude optional sessions. All four fields are whole INR per month including genuine zero, or cannot judge; no payment requested.
- S20: At what monthly price would it seem so inexpensive that you would doubt its quality?
- S21: At what monthly price would it seem inexpensive and good value?
- S22: At what monthly price would it start to seem expensive, but you would still consider it?
- S23: At what monthly price would it be so expensive that you would not consider it?

S24 optional open text: What, if anything, frustrates you most about online learning? “Nothing” allowed; no identifiers.
S25 optional open text: What would make a learning service worth paying for, if anything? “Would not pay” allowed; do not imply everyone wants a paid service.
S26 optional quote permission: May we quote a short passage anonymously after removing identifying details? Yes/No; default No. General consent is not quote permission.
