# Orbit — ten-slide TBI-GEU READ deck

## Overall story
Written for shortlisting judges reading the PDF and a five-minute pitch. Orbit proposes a learner-requested course loop, not another claim of an already proven catalog. The story moves from a lived problem and honest discovery gap to a concrete workflow, source-visible prototype, narrow market experiment, separate subscription/session economics, a measurable 90-day plan, team accountability and a cost-derived INR100,000 proposal. The single conclusion is: fund a bounded validation cycle, not assumed scale. Exactly ten main pages; no backups in this draft. All ten downstream pages replace the old14-slide layout. Numbers are published context, source observations or visibly estimated targets—not traction.
