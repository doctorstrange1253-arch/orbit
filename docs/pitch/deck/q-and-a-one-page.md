# TBI-GEU pitch — one-page Q&A

**Start with what is unproven, then explain the test.** This is a prototype pitch, not a launched-service claim.

**Who is first?** Proposed: consenting adult GEU students in selected years/branches who need one practical skill. Founder confirmation and the relevant official population are still missing.

**What customer proof exists?** Founder/friends experience only. No primary survey dataset or one-to-one interviews supplied. Report actual n, population, method, date and limits when collected. Targets are not results.

**Why pay rather than use YouTube?** Willingness to pay is unproven. Test relevant requested topics plus useful mentor feedback against free resources, using actual paid conversion after checkout is ready.

**Why will mentors join?** Not validated. Seek five genuine pilot commitments and two reviewed courses. An 80% mentor pool is shared among eligible enrolled-course mentors, not a guaranteed 80% payment to one mentor.

**What is built?** Source-visible requests/votes, finite journey UI and recorder/review controls. Historical screenshots use test data. Integrated commerce, advanced mentor waves and specialized AI remain next or unverified.

**How do you earn?** Subscriptions: 20% platform / 80% eligible mentor pool. Optional mentor-priced sessions: separate 15% platform fee. Core Rs 599 and Premium Rs 1,199 are proposed, not live prices.

**Break-even?** Illustrative 80% Core / 20% Premium scenario: Rs 63.36 contribution per learner, Rs 15,000 fixed monthly cost, 237 paying learners. Core-only: 296. Actual bills, tax basis and usage may change this.

**What does funding buy?** Proposed Rs 2 lakh pilot allocation, not supplier quotes or an award. First Rs 1 lakh: minimum pilot; next Rs 50,000: second course-launch cycle; final Rs 50,000: equipment/resilience. Borrow equipment and cut paid campaigns first.

**Runway?** Itemized funding is designated spending, not unrestricted cash. The model separates a three-month pilot allocation from hypothetical all-cash runway. Actual opening cash and bills remain missing.

**Moat, AI and safety?** No validated moat, learning lift, AI accuracy or compliance guarantee. Prove demand, mentor delivery, payment settlement and adult-pilot privacy/reliability before scaling.
