## How to read this inventory

- **Built and demo-able** requires proof of the exact demonstrated behavior. No current founder-operated or production demo was run in ST-0. Two narrow historical fixture observations are retained, but neither earns a blanket current-live status.
- **Partial** means source/wiring exists or a historical subset was observed, while integration, current demo or other acceptance is unverified. It does not mean every feature is half-built.
- **Planned** includes document-only extensions and unmounted implementation scaffolding. Such scaffolding is stated explicitly; Planned does not imply no code exists.
- **[OURS: source only]** identifies inspected repository evidence, not the stronger public tag "verified in code and demonstrable". Upgrade that public tag only after the founder can demonstrate the exact claim.
- **[FILL]** is a missing founder/runtime fact, not a zero value. **NOT_RUN/UNVERIFIED** is not success or failure.
- Plans, tests present in files and synthetic account values are not real users, revenue, adoption, completed learning or paid settlement. Historical fixtures below are dated 2026-11-09.
- Links are relative to this private document. Risk and Coach notes are internal preparation, not ready-to-paste competition answers.
