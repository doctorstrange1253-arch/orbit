## Explicit organizer warnings

- Spending the whole pitch on the problem and skipping economics/use of funds.
- Saying "everyone is our customer" instead of sharp ICP and GTM.
- Generic internet TAM without primary customer discovery.
- Funding ask without runway, milestones or itemized use.
- Rapid scalability promises without MVP, pilot or early traction.
- Hiding weak spots or claiming an unrealistic moat. The guidance says early student teams need not have everything figured out, and naming challenges builds trust.

These warnings do not authorize invented interviews, users, financial forecasts or source claims. Technical sophistication/3D/AI is not automatically customer validation or a defensible moat.
